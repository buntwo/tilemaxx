# English Word Prevalence Norms

The Casual vocabulary is adapted from Marc Brysbaert, Paweł Mandera, Samantha F. McCormick, and Emmanuel Keuleers (2019), *Word prevalence norms for 62,000 English lemmas*, Behavior Research Methods 51, 467–479. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-018-1077-9) · [Source workbook](https://osf.io/download/nbu9e/) · [Authors’ license notice](https://osf.io/download/z7fgj/)

Tilemaxx keeps alphabetic English spellings of 2–25 letters from the Prevalence sheet. For a spelling with more than one row, it uses the row with the most respondents. It keeps spellings that strictly more than 75% of respondents said they knew, then converts them to uppercase, sorts them, and compresses the newline-delimited list.

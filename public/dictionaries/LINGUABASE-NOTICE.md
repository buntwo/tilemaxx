# Linguabase familiarity scores

`linguabase-casual.csv.gz` is derived from the [full Linguabase vocabulary](https://www.linguabase.org/sample-data.html#vocabulary), published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). It contains two columns, `word` and `familiarity_score`.

The export converts words to uppercase letter spellings by joining spaces and dashes. Scored exact spellings take precedence. Published familiarity scores are scaled to 0–100, and the export includes scores of at least 90. It is generated independently of Tilemaxx game dictionaries.
